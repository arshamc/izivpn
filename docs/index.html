Privacy Policy & VpnService Disclosure

Application: izi vpn (com.arshadevelop.izivpn) • Effective Date: November 4, 2026

🔒 1. Strict Zero-Log Commitment

At izi vpn, your digital privacy is our absolute priority. We operate under a strict Zero-Log Policy. This means:

  • No Browsing Activity: We never monitor, record, store, or log the websites you visit, the content you access, or the files you download.
  • No Traffic Logs: We do not log source or destination IP addresses.
  • No Session Connection Metadata: We do not log connection timestamps, session durations, or bandwidth utilization linked to individual identities.
  • No DNS Logging: All DNS queries resolved inside the encrypted VPN tunnel are ephemeral and never persisted.

Because we do not collect or store your traffic data, we have nothing to share, sell, or disclose to any third party or governmental entity.

🚀 2. Google Play Prominent Disclosure & Android VpnService Usage

In accordance with Google Play's VpnService Policy, we explicitly disclose how and why our app uses Android's native VpnService API:

  • Purpose: izi vpn uses the Android VpnService to create an encrypted, system-level network tunnel using secure modern protocols (VLESS and Xray). This tunnel routes your device's Internet traffic securely to remote server endpoints.
  • User Benefit: The tunnel shields your network packets against unencrypted public Wi-Fi eavesdropping, man-in-the-middle (MITM) surveillance, and ISP packet throttling.
  • Scope of Operation: The VpnService runs strictly on your device to encrypt and forward traffic packets. It does not harvest personal credentials, inspect unencrypted payloads, or bypass user authorization.
  • Explicit User Consent: Before initiating any VPN tunnel, the app presents a clear prominent in-app disclosure dialog. The tunnel is established only after you explicitly tap "Agree & Continue" and approve Android's system VPN prompt.

📢 3. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)

To keep our high-speed server infrastructure accessible without mandatory subscriptions, izi vpn integrates Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) to serve non-intrusive advertisements (milestone interstitial and optional rewarded video ads).

Google AdMob may collect and process certain non-personally identifiable diagnostic information in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy, including:

  • Google Mobile Advertising ID (GAID / AAID).
  • Coarse device network indicators (such as country or IP geolocation).
  • App interaction and ad performance metrics (ad impressions, clicks).
  • Crash diagnostics and performance telemetry.

You can review Google's advertising practices and opt out of personalized ads at any time through Google's Privacy & Terms: https://policies.google.com/privacy, or via your Android device settings: Settings > Google > Ads > Delete Advertising ID.

🔑 4. Permissions Requested & Why

  • android.permission.BIND_VPN_SERVICE: Required by the Android OS to bind and manage the local VPN network interface (tun0).
  • android.permission.INTERNET: Required to communicate with remote proxy nodes and resolve network handshakes.
  • android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Required on Android 13 (API 33) and above to display the ongoing foreground service notification, allowing you to monitor connection status and disconnect at any time.

🛡️ 5. Security Architecture

We deploy end-to-end industry-grade cryptographic defenses to safeguard our service:

  • Remote server configurations are encrypted using AES-256-CBC payloads to prevent unauthorized scraping and tampering.
  • VPN network tunnels utilize modern TLS 1.3 cryptographic handshakes and Reality transport obfuscation.
